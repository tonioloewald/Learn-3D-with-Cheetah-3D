Rendering Reflected Lights

By default, Cheetah 3D’s lights are invisible objects. If you point a camera at them, you get nothing. Crucially: they don’t appear in reflections. (If you consider what a flashlight or a car’s headlight looks like in photographs or when you look at it, what you’re seeing is reflections of a glowing object.)

AI draft: generated from the Cheetah 3D 8.1.1 manual; not yet reviewed by the author.

Area lights have a Visible in camera option (older versions called it “geometry”) that makes the light’s shape visible in renders and in reflections, and the Sky Light can be drawn as the Background. The other lights simply have no such option. This doesn’t matter so much since the option doesn’t do a great job anyway — aside from the sky light, a visible light simply looks like an emissive rectangle or circle, and it’s not as bright as it should be (so it doesn’t look as bright in reflections as it should relative to reflections of objects that don’t shine).

Note: needs checking in Cheetah 3D 8.1.1: does the point light still have a Visible in camera (geometry) option, as the headlight example uses, and is a visible light still too dim in reflections, especially with Falcon? The manual documents Visible in camera only for area lights (manual: Objects › Light).
